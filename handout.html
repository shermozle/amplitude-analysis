Before you start

The demo site has a settings panel behind the gear button, bottom right. Don't put anything in the API key field. It redirects your events to a project nobody else can see, and you'll spend the session wondering why your charts are empty.

Also in that panel: a toggle labelled Guides & Surveys. It's wired to the SDK's opt-out flag, so switching it off stops the site sending anything, replay included, with no error. If your events vanish, check it first.

One project, two sources

Read this before you build anything

The sandbox receives data from two places at once, and knowing which is which will save you most of the confusion in this session.

SourceWhat it isWhat's in it
Generated history About a month of backdated behaviour, so charts have something to show. 19 of the 40 events. Telco journeys, but a fashion catalogue in the cart line items, which can't be configured out.
The live site Browser SDK, autocapture and Session Replay on. Your clicks land within seconds. All 40 events. Telstra devices, plans and accessories. Today only, because it starts when we start.

The two sources agree on names, not on contents. Nearly every odd number you'll meet today comes back to that.

The exercises

Self-paced · each one has a "done when" so you know to move on

Ex 1

Make some data

3 min
  1. Stay logged out. While you're anonymous your device ID is yours alone, which is what makes you findable in exercise 2.
  2. Open a mobile phone on a plan. Pick a colour and a storage size, choose a plan, add it to the cart.
  3. In the cart, apply the promo code ONLINE50.
  4. Check out. Four steps, any values in the form fields.
Done whenYou've placed an order, and you can name three events you think you just sent.
Ex 2

Follow it through the three tools

6 min
  1. Event Explorer (Chrome extension), or the site's own log at Ctrl+Shift+D if you haven't installed it. Find your Order Placed and open the Products property. One object per line item.
  2. Check that discount_applied came through as Yes, and work out which of your actions caused that.
  3. Ingestion Debugger, in Amplitude under Data. Find the same event arriving at the server.
  4. User Lookup. Search for user ID 2003964661. Then log in on the site, as anyone, and look again.
Done whenYou can explain what the Ingestion Debugger tells you that the extension doesn't, and why the user profile in step 4 contains the whole room.
Ex 3

Data Table

5 min
  1. New chart, Data Table. Rows: Add to Cart, Checkout Started, Order Placed.
  2. Add a second metric so you have uniques and totals side by side. Look at where they diverge.
  3. Break down by Products.department: Mobile, Internet, Accessories.
  4. Add a segment for users whose telstra_plus_tier is Gold or VIP.
Done whenYou can say which department Gold and VIP members add to cart most, and whether they check out more often than everyone else.
Ex 4

Funnel

5 min
  1. New chart, Funnel: Product Viewed → Add to Cart → Checkout Started → Order Placed.
  2. Change the conversion window from 1 day to 30 days. Watch the rate move: same data, different question.
  3. Group by Products.department.
  4. Insert Checkout Step Completed as step 4, and watch the funnel collapse.
Done whenYou can explain why adding a step that obviously happens dropped conversion to nearly zero.
Ex 5

Journeys and User Sessions

4 min
  1. New chart, Journeys. Start from Address Checked, filtered to the results where serviceability failed. Read the branches out.
  2. New chart, User Sessions. Sessions per user and average session length.
  3. Add a second series segmented to people who placed an order, and compare.
Done whenYou can name the two most common next steps after a failed address check, and say whether purchasers take more sessions than everyone else.
Ex 6

Your chart

15 min
  1. Before you touch Amplitude, write your question in the worksheet below. One sentence. Something you actually get asked in Adobe.
  2. Build one chart that answers it. Any of the four types, any of this data.
  3. If it can't be answered here, write down what's missing: which event, which property, on which step.
Done whenYou have a chart you'd show a stakeholder, or one line describing the gap that stopped you. The gap is the more useful of the two.

The four chart types

And the Adobe route you'd normally take for the same question

The questionChartUsual Adobe routeWhere it differs
How many, broken down by whatData TableFreeform table Any property is available without being configured first, because there are no slots to allocate.
Where do people fall outFunnelFallout You set the conversion window and the step-order rule explicitly, and both change the answer.
What did they do nextJourneysFlow Starts from any event, not just a page, so a path can begin at a failed address check.
How often, for how longUser SessionsVisits metrics A session is a gap of inactivity, set once per project, rather than a hit-based visit.

Amplitude asks you to pick the question type before it gives you a canvas. Coming from a blank freeform table that feels like a constraint, and mostly it isn't: the chart knows what you're asking, so the controls in front of you are the ones that question needs.

When a number looks wrong

Four things in this data that will make you think something's broken

What you'll seeWhat's actually happening
dicount_applied and discount_applied, both real The generator misspells it, missing an S. The site spells it correctly. Two children under one parent, each populated for roughly half the data. Group by either and you've quietly reported on half the cohort. Nothing errors.
Fossil and Michael Kors next to iPhones The generated line items carry a hardcoded fashion catalogue. Scope the chart to today and it resolves to Telstra devices. Two sources agreeing on a schema and disagreeing on contents.
Page Viewed and [Amplitude] Page Viewed Autocapture sends one, the site sends its own with more properties. Neither is wrong. Someone in a hurry picks the wrong one and the numbers are close enough that nobody notices.
An event with no data at all 21 of the 40 events are live only, so they look empty until someone uses the site. A funnel containing one of them silently narrows to the handful of people who have every step.
The habit

The Products array

Four events carry a Products object array, one object per line item, which Amplitude splits into child properties after ingestion. Those children are what appear in a group-by, and they're what let you group by something inside the cart rather than something about the cart as a whole.

Child propertyValues
Products.brandApple, Samsung, Google, Nokia, Telstra — plus the fashion brands from the generated history
Products.departmentMobile, Internet, Accessories
Products.categoriesSmartphone, nbn, 5G Home, SIM Only, Audio, Charging, Home, Protection
Products.discount_appliedYes, No — and note Products.dicount_applied sitting next to it
Products.item_idinteger · 101–112 devices, 201–206 accessories, 301–310 internet, 401–403 plan tiers
Products.pricenumber · monthly charge on plans, one-off charge on accessories. Product mix questions, not revenue questions.
Products.quantityalways 1 · the site has no quantity stepper
Why price and revenue are kept apart

A Telstra cart mixes recurring and one-off charges: an iPhone on a plan at $120 a month next to a $399 pair of AirPods. Summing one price field across those is meaningless in either direction, so Order Placed carries total_monthly and total_upfront as separate properties. Every subscription business hits this, and most first attempts pick one field and lose the distinction.

Seven questions that work in this data

If your own question isn't coming, take one of these

ChartQuestion, and how to build it
Data TableWhich device brand gets added to cart most? Break Add to Cart down by Products.brand, scoped to today.
Data TableWhat share of orders carry trade-in credit? Order Placed, broken down by trade_in_credit.
FunnelWhich checkout step loses the most people? Steps from Checkout Started onwards, live data only.
FunnelDo Gold and VIP members convert better than Members? Group the purchase funnel by telstra_plus_tier.
FunnelDoes a promo code change the order rate? Promo Code Applied → Order Placed.
JourneysWhat do people do after checking their usage? Start from Usage Checked.
User SessionsHow many sessions before someone orders? Sessions per user, segmented on having placed an order.

Magic strings

The handful of literals you'll need

  • ONLINE50 and PROMO20 are the two working promo codes. Anything else returns an invalid result, which is itself a usable event to analyse.
  • 2003964661 is the customer ID that every logged-in visitor becomes, because the site hardcodes it. One profile, all of us.
  • Ctrl+Shift+D opens the site's demo panel. Ctrl+Shift+R resets its demo data.
  • Login accepts any email and any password.
